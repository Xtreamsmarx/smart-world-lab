Advanced Intelligence Technology (AIT) Transfer Center

A university-wide coordination layer that helps UND adopt AI safely, share AI capability across units, and turn research breakthroughs into practical daily impact.

Director Iraj H.P. Mamaghani, Professor, Civil Engineering
Contact iraj.mamaghani@und.edu

Executive Summary in 60 Seconds

AI is changing teaching, research, and operations. The AIT Transfer Center makes that change coordinated, safe, and useful across all UND units.

Bridge Layer

Connects AI research, IT, and teaching into one coordinated adoption system.

Responsible AI

Validation, compliance, and lifecycle monitoring before and after deployment.

Campus Enablement

Mentors and support teams help faculty, staff, and students use AI effectively.

Continuous Transfer

Inward, internal, and outward AI transfer aligned with UND goals and impact.

AIT Transfer Model

One flow, three transfer directions, continuous value creation.

Inward Transfer

Adopt new AI capabilities into courses, labs, and operations.

Internal Transfer

Share models, workflows, and practices across departments.

Outward Transfer

Collaborate responsibly with industry, agencies, and community.

Model-Agnostic AI Gateway

The AIT Gateway is UND's central AI integration and control layer.

  • Validate: check model quality and readiness
  • Safety: assess risks and safeguards
  • Compliance: policy and governance alignment
  • Performance: benchmark real utility
  • Monitor: continuous lifecycle oversight
