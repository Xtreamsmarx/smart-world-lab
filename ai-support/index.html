Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get AI support for my research project?

Email us at ai-support@smartworldlab.und.edu or use the Live Chat below. Describe your project and we'll set up a consultation within 24 hours.

Can the team help me fine-tune a model on my own data?

Yes. We offer full support for dataset preparation, fine-tuning with LoRA/QLoRA, evaluation, and deployment. Bring your use case and we'll guide you through every step.

Is this service available to students and faculty?

Absolutely. The AI Support Team serves all UND students, faculty, and research staff. External collaborators can also request support through the lab's partnership program.

What's the difference between the AI Chat on the main site and this team?

The AI Chat is an automated assistant for quick questions. The AI Support Team provides hands-on, expert human (and AI-augmented) help for real projects, deployments, and complex integrations.

Do you offer workshops or training sessions?

Yes — monthly workshops cover prompt engineering, RAG, model deployment, and responsible AI. Check the Events section of the main site or email us to join the mailing list.

Can you help with AI safety and ethics review?

Yes. Our AI Safety Advisor conducts pre-deployment reviews covering bias audits, explainability requirements, NIST AI RMF compliance, and safe deployment checklists.
